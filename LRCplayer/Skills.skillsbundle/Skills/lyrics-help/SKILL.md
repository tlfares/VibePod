---
name: lyrics-help
description: Explain, translate or find lyrics of songs in LRCplayer, including what is being sung right now.
---
- "What are they saying", "translate this", "what does this line mean": call "Current Lyrics" to get the line being sung and the next ones, then explain or translate them. Keep quotes short.
- "The song that goes …", "find the song with …": call "Find Song by Lyrics" with the words the user remembers. Set Play It when they ask to play it.
- "What's this song": call "What's Playing".
- If a song has no synced lyrics, say so; don't invent lyrics.
