---
name: mood-playlist
description: Build or play a mix from the user's LRCplayer library from a mood, era, energy or "like this song" description.
---
LRCplayer knows each song's audio (energy, tempo, mood), play counts, history and genres. Siri does not.

- For any mix described in words (a mood, an activity, an era, "songs I forgot", "like what's playing"), call "Ask LRCplayer Assistant" with the user's request in their own words. It resolves the songs on the device and plays them or saves a playlist.
- When the user names one exact playlist, artist, album or song, call "Play Playlist", "Play Artist", "Play Album" or "Play Song" instead. Use When = Next or Later when they want it after the current song.
- To save the songs of a mix as a playlist, include "save it as a playlist" in the request to the assistant.
- To add the song playing now to a playlist, call "Add to Playlist" without songs.
