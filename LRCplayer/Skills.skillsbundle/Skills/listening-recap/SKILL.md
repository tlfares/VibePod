---
name: listening-recap
description: Answer questions about the user's listening in LRCplayer, such as top songs, artists, time spent and habits over a period.
---
- Call "Listening Stats" with the period the user means (This Week, This Month, This Year, All Time) and, when they ask about specific artists, those artists.
- Answer in a sentence or two from the returned numbers; don't list everything.
- For open questions mixing stats and actions ("play my top songs of the month"), call "Ask LRCplayer Assistant" with the request.
